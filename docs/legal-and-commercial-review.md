# Pushkey Legal And Commercial Review

Status: Phase 11 launch-policy draft.

## Terms Checklist

- Define the launch product scope and beta/deferred features.
- State that users are responsible for preserving master passwords and recovery
  codes.
- State that cloud sync, when enabled, stores encrypted vault blobs and metadata,
  not plaintext secrets.
- Define acceptable use, prohibited abuse, suspension, and termination.
- Define liability limits, warranty disclaimers, and governing terms.

## Licensing And Refunds

- Paid plans must match server-authoritative tier enforcement.
- Refund rules must state the refund window, excluded abuse cases, and support
  contact path.
- License transfer, cancellation, renewal, and device-limit behavior must be
  documented before paid launch.

## Privacy Review Inputs

Use [data-governance.md](data-governance.md) as the source for:

- account metadata;
- license and device metadata;
- support records;
- encrypted vault blobs;
- logs, metrics, audit records, and error reports;
- retention, export, deletion, and subprocessors.

## Support Ownership

- Publish the primary support contact.
- Assign severity owners and response targets.
- Keep security reports private until coordinated disclosure is complete.
- Maintain an incident communication template and status-page process.
